              Centering checker

              Photograph a card straight on, on a dark surface. HoloValue finds the borders. Drag any line to fine-tune it, and you’ll see the centering and the best PSA grade it allows.

              Binder page scan

              Take one photo of a whole binder page or a row of cards laid out on a table. HoloValue finds every card and its price.

              Tip: fill the photo with the page, hold the phone flat above it, and tilt the binder to avoid glare.
